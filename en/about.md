**UnificarPDF** is a free web platform that brings together the essential tools for working with PDF documents: merging files, splitting them and converting JPG images to PDF, all from your browser and without installing any software.

## 1. Who We Are

UnificarPDF started with a simple idea: handling a PDF should not require expensive software, complicated steps or technical knowledge. We are an independent project focused on providing clear, fast and accessible tools for everyone, whether you are a student, a professional or a business.

## 2. Our Mission

To make everyday work with documents easier through free, reliable and easy-to-use tools, available in each user's own language.

## 3. What We Offer

* **Merge PDF:** combine several PDF files into a single document in the order you choose.
* **Split PDF:** separate a PDF into multiple files or extract only the pages you need.
* **JPG to PDF:** convert one or more JPG images into a PDF document ready to share, send or print.

## 4. Why Choose UnificarPDF

* **Free:** you can use our tools at no cost.
* **Simple:** a clear interface that gets you the result in just a few steps.
* **No installation:** it works directly in your browser, on a computer, tablet or mobile phone.
* **Multilingual:** the site is available in several languages so you can work comfortably.
* **Respect for your privacy:** your files are not stored permanently.

## 5. Privacy and Security

Files are processed only to provide the requested service and are not permanently stored on UnificarPDF. We do not claim ownership of your content. For more information, please read our Privacy Policy and our Terms of Use.

## 6. Available in Your Language

UnificarPDF is available in Spanish, English, French, Portuguese, Thai, Indonesian, German, Italian, Turkish and Hungarian. We keep expanding the site to reach more users around the world.
